About Us

The Geneva-London Axis

Founded in 2006, Prestinvest SA is a FINMA-regulated external asset manager headquartered in Geneva. We bridge the trust and discretion of Swiss private banking with the analytical power of quantitative innovation.

Our core team in Geneva is backed by senior London-based advisors from Barclays, BNP Paribas, and Dresdner — delivering global institutional expertise at boutique scale. We are your trusted and privileged partner for above-average risk-adjusted returns.
